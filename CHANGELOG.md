## 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* `LiquidGlassNavBar`: floating glass capsule bottom bar with a stretchy, draggable selection pill.
* `LiquidGlassNavRail`: the same look as a vertical side rail for tablets and desktop.
* `LiquidGlassScaffold`: switches between bar and rail by width and lets content scroll behind the glass.
* `LiquidGlassMode.auto`, `glass` and `frosted` rendering, themed from your `ColorScheme`.
* `LiquidGlassStyle` for colours, blur, sizes, margins and animation timing.
* Badges, haptics, labels that hide when space is tight, and full semantics.
